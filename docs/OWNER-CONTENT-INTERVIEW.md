## SEO cheat sheet (read this first)

### The 5 content pillars we're filling

| # | Pillar | What it becomes on the site |
|---|--------|------------------------------|
| 1 | **Identity** | Hero bullets, "Our Identity" section, About page — who you are, not price gimmicks |
| 2 | **Why Us** | Trust cards, team, process, reviews — why Modesto homeowners & builders choose Elite |

| 4 | **Service Areas** | One dedicated page per city (1,000+ words each) — highest-ROI SEO pages |
| 5 | **Blog** | Educational articles that link back to services + cities |

### H1 + H2 geo-keyword formula

**Rule:** One **H1** per page with primary keyword + city/region. **H2s** support with secondary keywords + geo — natural language, not stuffed.

| Page type | H1 pattern (example) | H2 pattern (examples) |
|-----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| **Homepage** | Expert Home Framing in **Modesto** & Northern California | Residential Framing Services in the **Central Valley** · Why **Modesto** Homeowners Trust Elite Builders |
| **Service (Framing)** | Framing Contractor in **Modesto**, CA | Residential Framing in **Modesto** · Commercial Framing Across the **Central Valley** |
| **Service Area (city)** | Framing Contractor in **[City]**, CA | Framing Services We Offer in **[City]** · Neighborhoods We Serve in **[City]** · Recent Projects in **[City]** |
| **Blog** | How Much Does House Framing Cost in **Modesto**? | Factors That Affect Framing Cost in the **Central Valley** · When to Hire a Framing Contractor in **Modesto** |
| **About** | About Elite Builders — **Modesto** Framing Contractor Since 2012 | Our Story in the **Central Valley** · What Integrity Means on Every Job Site |

**SEO title format:** `[Primary Keyword] [City] CA | Elite Builders IX`  
**Meta description:** 1–2 sentences with service + city + CTA ("free estimate").

**20/20/60 content mix (from strategy notes):**
- ~20% — who you are / identity / differentiation
- ~20% — trust (reviews, license, team, process)
- ~60% — useful info (services, local context, FAQs, project details)

## Section 2 — Identity pillar

**Site sections:** Hero intro, hero bullets (Who We Are / How We're Different / What To Expect), "Our Identity" cards.

### Current identity framework (validate or rewrite in his voice)

**Three hero messages:**
1. **Who We Are** — Structure first — no gimmicks or hidden fees.
2. **How We're Different** — Honest estimates, clear communication, crews you trust.
3. **What To Expect** — Straight answers and work we'd stand behind.

**Three identity pillars:**
1. **No Cutting Corners** — Frame to code and spec; no shortcuts or cover-ups.
2. **Integrity** — Do the right thing even when you didn't have to.
3. **Promises** — Show up, communicate honestly, stand behind timeline and scope.

### Questions to ask

**Origin story**
- [ ] Walk me through how Elite Builders started. What was happening in your life around 2012?
- [ ] You mention faith became foundational — how does that show up on a job site day to day? (OK to quote?)
- [ ] What's a moment early on that defined how you'd run the company?

**Who We Are**
- [ ] Finish this: "We're not a ___ — we'r e a ___."
- [ ] What do competitors do that you **refuse** to do? (hidden fees, change orders, ghosting, cutting corners?)
- [ ] What would you want a homeowner to know in the first 10 seconds?

**How We're Different**
- [ ] Why do GCs and homeowners call **you** back after the first job?
- [ ] Give me a specific example of clear communication saving a project.
- [ ] What's your estimate process? Walk-through, plans review, timeline — step by step.

**What To Expect**
- [ ] From first call to final walkthrough — what should the customer expect?
- [ ] How often do you update the client during a frame?
- [ ] What happens when something goes wrong? Real example.

**No Cutting Corners**
- [ ] Tell me about a time you fixed something that wasn't in scope because it was the right thing.
- [ ] What does "passing inspection the first time" mean to your crew?
- [ ] Any framing detail you're especially proud of? (shear panels, hardware, trusses, etc.)

**Integrity**
- [ ] His definition of integrity — in his own words (we have a draft; does this sound like him?):
  > "Integrity means doing the right thing even when you didn't have to — because it's the right thing to do."
- [ ] Have you ever turned down a job because it wasn't the right fit? What happened?
- [ ] Have you ever told a client bad news they needed to hear? What was it?

**Promises**
- [ ] What promises do you make on every job? (show up time, cleanup, timeline, warranty?)
- [ ] What's your warranty or callback policy?
- [ ] A time you kept a promise when it cost you extra — what happened?

- [ ] **H1 candidate:** About Elite Builders — Modesto Framing Contractor Since [YEAR]
